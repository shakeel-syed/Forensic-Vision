# Pilot Strategy: AAA & Farmers Insurance
## Modernizing Claims & Roadside Assistance with AI Quality Gates

### 1. The Core Problem for Tier-1 Insurers
For companies like **AAA** or **Farmers**, "Digital Self-Service" is their biggest cost-saver. However, if a customer uploads a blurry photo of a car accident or a VIN number:
*   **AAA:** A tow truck might be sent with the wrong equipment because the damage wasn't clear.
*   **Farmers:** An adjuster can't approve a "Quick Pay" claim, leading to a frustrated customer and a manual phone call (which costs ~$15-$25 per interaction).

### 2. The Solution: "Instant Approval" AI
Your application acts as a "Virtual Adjuster" sitting on the customer's phone, giving them instant feedback.

---

### 3. Pilot Implementation Plan (The 30-Day Trial)

#### Phase 1: Integration (Week 1)
*   **Mechanism:** Farmers/AAA integrates your `ForensicAPI.py` into their mobile app's "Photo Upload" button.
*   **A/B Test:** 50% of users get the standard "Upload" button. 50% get your "AI-Validated Upload."

#### Phase 2: Active Monitoring (Weeks 2-3)
*   **Data Collection:** Track how many times your AI issues a "Retry" and if the second photo is successfully "Approved."
*   **Human Validation:** A small team of adjusters reviews the "Rejected" photos to confirm the AI was correct.

#### Phase 3: Value Assessment (Week 4)
*   Compare the "Time-to-Claim-Approval" for the AI-assisted group vs. the control group.

---

### 4. Why AAA & Farmers will say "YES"
*   **NPS Score (Customer Satisfaction):** Customers hate being told 3 days later that their photo was bad. Instant feedback feels like better service.
*   **Fraud Prevention:** High-clarity photos make it much harder for someone to hide pre-existing damage with "accidental" blur.
*   **Operational Savings:** If your tool prevents just 500 "re-submission" phone calls a month, it pays for itself 10x over.

---

### 5. Pitching the "Low-Risk" Entry
When approaching their innovation teams, use these three points:
1.  **Non-Disruptive:** "We don't replace your claims system; we just clean the data entering it."
2.  **Privacy-First:** "Photos are analyzed in memory and can be processed in your own secure Google Cloud VPC (BYOK)."
3.  **Model Efficiency:** "We use Gemini 2.5 Flash to keep the cost per claim under $0.01."

---

### 6. Recommended Next Steps for the Pilot
*   **Demo Case:** Use the `Dashboard.py` to show them a "Farmers Branded" version of the upload process.
*   **Performance Report:** Use the `Inspection_report.md` generated by the system to show the level of detail the AI provides to the adjuster.
